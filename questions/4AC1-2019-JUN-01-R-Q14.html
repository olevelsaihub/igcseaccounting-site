  1. Part (a) · 5 marks · Prepare · Updating the cash book from the bank statement · A-Grade Plan · practice
    Read part (a) as text

    (a) Prepare an updated cash book at 30 April 2019. Balance the cash book on that date and bring the balance down on 1 May 2019. (5)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (a)

    Enter the opening balance on the correct side (an overdraft is a credit). Add only items on the bank statement not yet in the cash book: receipts on the debit side, payments and charges on the credit side. Balance and bring down with the date; no timing differences.

    Hint for part (b)

    Head it with the date and start from the updated cash book balance (brackets for an overdraft). Add unpresented cheques, deduct outstanding lodgements, and label the result 'Balance per bank statement'. Check it matches the statement.
