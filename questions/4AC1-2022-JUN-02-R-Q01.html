  2. Part (a)(ii) · 4 marks · Prepare · Preparing a manufacturer's income statement · A-Grade Plan · practice
    Read part (a)(ii) as text

    (ii) Prepare the income statement for the year ended 31 March 2022 showing the trading section only. (4)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (a)(ii)

    Use finished goods inventory and production cost in cost of sales, not raw materials or work in progress. Adjust shared expenses for accruals and prepayments first, then take only the office share. Production wages belong in the manufacturing account.
