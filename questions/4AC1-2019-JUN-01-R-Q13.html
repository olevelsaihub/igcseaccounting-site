    13 On 31 March 2019 Ehab sold machinery for $16 000. The machinery had been purchased on 1 November 2016 for $24 000. He depreciates machinery at 20% per annum using the reducing balance method. A full year’s depreciation is charged in the year of purchase but none in the year of disposal. Ehab’s financial year end is 31 March. Prepare the disposal account.     Hint for part (whole)

    In the disposal account, debit the asset's cost and credit both the depreciation on it and the proceeds; the balancing figure is the profit or loss, detail 'Income statement'. Work out the sold asset's depreciation using the time policy, and in any cost account remove only the cost, not the carrying value.
