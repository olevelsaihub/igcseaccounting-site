    (ii) Prepare the statement of financial position at 31 December 2023. (15)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (a)(ii)

    Find opening equity from the opening assets and liabilities, then build equity: add new capital and profit, deduct drawings (including goods taken). Depreciate every asset held, classify each loan by when it is repaid, and show the overdraft as a current liability.
