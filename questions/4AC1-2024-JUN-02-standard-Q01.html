  1. Part (a)(i) · 15 marks · Prepare · Preparing an income statement for a trader or service business · A-Grade Plan · practice
    Read part (a)(i) as text

    (a) (i) Prepare the income statement for the year ended 31 March 2024. (15)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (a)(i)

    Work through the additional information first, noting each adjustment next to the balance it affects. Then lay out revenue, cost of sales, gross profit, other income, expenses and profit for the year with a full heading. Watch prepayments and accruals, carriage inwards versus outwards, and depreciation.

  2. Part (a)(ii) · 5 marks · Prepare · Partnership appropriation account · A-Grade Plan · practice
    Read part (a)(ii) as text

    (ii) Prepare the appropriation account for the year ended 31 March 2024. (5)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (a)(ii)

    Write the heading and start with profit for the year, add total interest on drawings, then deduct interest on capital and salaries (monthly figure x 12). Share the residual profit in the agreed ratio, never the original profit, and check the split adds up.
