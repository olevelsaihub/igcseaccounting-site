    Hint for part (a)

    For each balance, ask: asset, expense or drawings (debit) or liability, equity, income or provision (credit)? Watch tricky ones: overdraft, provisions, returns inwards and outwards, discounts. Add both columns and enter any difference as 'Suspense' on the smaller side.
