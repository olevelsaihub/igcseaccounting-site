    17 Markus maintains a three-column cash book. On 1 December 2019 the balances were: bank $340 overdrawn, cash $70 The following transactions took place in December 2019. December Transaction 4 Received a cheque from Patel, a credit customer, $125 5 Sold goods for cash, $2 350 12 Paid cash into the bank, $1300 Received notice from the bank that the cheque from Patel, $125, had been 13 dishonoured. 19 Paid by standing order to Westgas, $85 Received a cheque from Sumner, $1140, in full settlement of an invoice after 23 taking 5% cash discount. 28 Paid wages by cash, $840 Paid Linston by cheque in full settlement of an invoice for $1 420. Payment 29 was after taking 10% cash discount. Enter the transactions in the three-column cash book on page 17. Balance the cash book at 31 December 2019 and bring down the balances at 1 January 2020. 16 Bank $ (Total for Question 17 = 15 marks) Cash $ Discount received $

    Hint for part (whole)

    Start with the opening balances on the correct side (watch for an overdraft), then enter each transaction on the right side and column with its date and details. Record discounts on the settlement line, enter contras twice, and balance cash and bank separately, bringing them down next month.
