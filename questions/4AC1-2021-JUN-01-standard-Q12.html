    Hint for part (whole)

    Enter opening balances b/d on the right side (expense: owing on credit, prepaid on debit; reverse for income), then the cash with detail 'Bank'. Carry down closing balances, find the balancing figure labelled 'Income statement', and bring balances down on the opposite side on the first day of next year.
