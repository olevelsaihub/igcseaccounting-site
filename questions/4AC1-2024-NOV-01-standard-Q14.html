    Read part (c) as text

    (c) Prepare the travel expenses account for the month of March 2024. Balance the account on 31 March 2024 and bring the balance down on 1 April 2024. (3)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (c)

    Put the opening balance on its normal side, then post each book total to that same side with the date and the book's name. Leave out returns, and close the account the way the question says: transfer to the income statement, or balance c/d and b/d.

    Read part (e) as text

    (e) Prepare the updated bank account at 31 March 2024. Balance the account on this date and bring the balance down on 1 April 2024. (5) Bank Account Date Details $ Date Details $ Mar 31 Mar 31 (Total for Question 14 = 15 marks)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (e)

    Enter the opening balance on the correct side (an overdraft is a credit). Add only items on the bank statement not yet in the cash book: receipts on the debit side, payments and charges on the credit side. Balance and bring down with the date; no timing differences.
