  4. Part (c) · 4 marks · Calculate · Calculating depreciation and carrying value · A-Grade Plan · worked example
    Read part (c) as text

    (c) Calculate the depreciation charge for each machine for each year. (4)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (c)

    Write down cost, residual value, rate or life, method and the time policy, then list each financial year. Straight line gives the same charge every year; reducing balance applies the rate to the carrying value at the start of each year. Check whether the question wants accumulated depreciation or carrying value, and show workings with $.

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (e)

    This account normally has a credit balance: opening balance b/d and the year's charge go on the credit side, depreciation on assets sold goes on the debit side as 'Disposal'. Use 'Income statement' as the detail for the year's charge, follow the time policy, and bring the balance down on the first day of next year.
