    (b) Calculate the balance of the machinery‑cost account at 1 July 2024. (1)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (b)

    Find the asset's cost, work out its depreciation up to the date of sale using the time policy, and compare carrying value with the proceeds. Say clearly whether the result is a profit or a loss. For a cost account balance, remember to add purchases and take off only the cost of assets sold.

  3. Part (c) · 5 marks · Prepare · Disposal of non-current assets: cost, disposal and profit or loss on sale · A-Grade Plan · practice
    Read part (c) as text

    (c) Prepare the following accounts for the year ended 31 December 2024. (5)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (c)

    In the disposal account, debit the asset's cost and credit both the depreciation on it and the proceeds; the balancing figure is the profit or loss, detail 'Income statement'. Work out the sold asset's depreciation using the time policy, and in any cost account remove only the cost, not the carrying value.
