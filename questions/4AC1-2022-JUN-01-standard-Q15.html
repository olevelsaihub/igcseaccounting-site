  2. Part (b)(i) · 6 marks · Prepare · Writing up customer and supplier accounts · A-Grade Plan · practice
    Read part (b)(i) as text

    (b) (i) Prepare the account of Rafa for the month of April 2022. Balance the account on 30 April 2022 and bring the balance down on 1 May 2022. (6)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (b)(i)

    Decide if the person is a customer (opening balance on the debit side) or a supplier (credit side). Use amounts after trade discount, check whether cash discount is allowed, post each item with date and the other account's name, then balance and bring down on the opposite side.

  3. Part (b)(ii) · 1 mark · State · Where does this item go in the financial statements? · A-Grade Plan · practice
    Read part (b)(ii) as text

    (ii) State in which section of the statement of financial position at 30 April 2022 the balance of Rafa’s account would appear. (1)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (b)(ii)

    Name the exact section, for example current assets or non-current liabilities, not just 'assets'. For a bank balance, check the side: a debit is money held, a credit is an overdraft.
