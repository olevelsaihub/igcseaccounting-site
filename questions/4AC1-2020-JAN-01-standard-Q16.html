    Read part (a) as text

    (a) Prepare the trade payables ledger control account for the month ended 30 November 2019. Balance the account on this date and bring the balances down at 1 December 2019. (10)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (a)

    Enter both opening balances b/d separately, cross out items that do not belong (cash sales, the other ledger's items), and post each item to the side that increases or decreases the debt. Carry down any unusual-side balance first, find the main balance, and bring both down on the next day.

  2. Part (b) · 1 mark · State · Reading the balance on a control account · Grade 8 Plan · practice
    Read part (b) as text

    (b) State one possible reason why Omar has a debit balance on the trade payables ledger control account. (1)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (b)

    Check which side the balance is on and which ledger. For an unusual balance, think about how the debt could be 'turned round', such as paying too much or returning goods after paying. One short reason per mark.
