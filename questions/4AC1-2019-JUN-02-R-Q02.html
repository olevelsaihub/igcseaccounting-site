    Read part (c) as text

    (c) Complete the table, by indicating with a tick (✓) the effect each transaction would have on the profit for the year of a manufacturer. (3)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (c)

    Write the double entry for each item or correction. If neither account is income or expense, there is no effect on profit; expenses up or income down means profit falls. Watch drawings and non-current asset purchases, which do not change profit.
