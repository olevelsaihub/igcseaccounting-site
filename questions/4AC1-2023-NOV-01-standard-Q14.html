    Read part (b) as text

    (b) Prepare the account of Tina. Balance the account on 30 April 2023 and bring the balance down on 1 May 2023. (6)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (b)

    Decide if the person is a customer (opening balance on the debit side) or a supplier (credit side). Use amounts after trade discount, check whether cash discount is allowed, post each item with date and the other account's name, then balance and bring down on the opposite side.
