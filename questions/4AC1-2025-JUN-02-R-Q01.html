    Hint for part (a)

    Work through the additional information first, noting each adjustment next to the balance it affects. Then lay out revenue, cost of sales, gross profit, other income, expenses and profit for the year with a full heading. Watch prepayments and accruals, carriage inwards versus outwards, and depreciation.

  2. Part (b) · 4 marks · Calculate · Calculating a revised profit · Grade 9 Plan · practice
    Read part (b) as text

    (b) Calculate the adjusted profit for the year ended 31 December 2024 after the correction of this error. (4)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (b)

    Start with the draft profit, then put each adjustment on its own line, deciding whether it adds or removes income or an expense. Only include the part that belongs to this year, bracket every deduction, and label your final figure as the revised profit for the year.
