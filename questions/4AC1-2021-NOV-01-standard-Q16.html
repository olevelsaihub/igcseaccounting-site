    (a) Prepare the trade receivables ledger control account for the month ended 31 August 2020. Balance the account on this date and bring the balances down at 1 September 2020. (10)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (a)

    Enter both opening balances b/d separately, cross out items that do not belong (cash sales, the other ledger's items), and post each item to the side that increases or decreases the debt. Carry down any unusual-side balance first, find the main balance, and bring both down on the next day.
