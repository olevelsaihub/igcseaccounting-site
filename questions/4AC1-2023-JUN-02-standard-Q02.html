  3. Part (c) · 12 marks · Prepare · Partnership appropriation account · A-Grade Plan · practice
    Read part (c) as text

    (c) Prepare the appropriation account for the year ended 31 December 2022. (12)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (c)

    Write the heading and start with profit for the year, add total interest on drawings, then deduct interest on capital and salaries (monthly figure x 12). Share the residual profit in the agreed ratio, never the original profit, and check the split adds up.
