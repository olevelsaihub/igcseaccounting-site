    15 On 30 April 2025, a trader identified the following errors. The purchase of a motor vehicle had been entered in the motor 1 expenses account. A payment to George, a credit supplier, had been recorded on the debit side 2 of the bank account and the credit side of George’s account. 3 A credit sale to Dalton had been posted to David’s account. 4 No entries had been made for goods taken by the owner for their own use. 5 A sales invoice, $320, had been recorded as $230 in the sales day book. Complete the table below to indicate the type of error and the double entry required to correct each error.     Hint for part (whole)

    Write what was done and what should have been done, then the adjustment needed. If only one account is wrong, the suspense account is the other leg. Use the difference for wrong amounts and double the figure for wrong-side postings.
