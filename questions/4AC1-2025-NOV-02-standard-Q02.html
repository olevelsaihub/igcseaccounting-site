    Work through the additional information first, noting each adjustment next to the balance it affects.     Hint for part (b)

    Write a full heading with the date, then show non-current assets at cost less accumulated depreciation that includes this year's charge. List current assets with trade receivables after the provision for irrecoverable debts, and if equity and liabilities are asked, check the two totals agree.
