  2. Part (b) · 9 marks · Prepare · Disposal of non-current assets: cost, disposal and profit or loss on sale · A-Grade Plan · worked example
    Read part (b) as text

    (b) Prepare the following accounts for the year ended 31 December 2022. (9)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (b)

    In the disposal account, debit the asset's cost and credit both the depreciation on it and the proceeds; the balancing figure is the profit or loss, detail 'Income statement'. Work out the sold asset's depreciation using the time policy, and in any cost account remove only the cost, not the carrying value.

    Read part (c)(i) as text

    (c) (i) Identify, indicating with a tick (), the effect on gross profit if the purchase of the new vehicle was treated as revenue expenditure. (1)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (c)(i)

    Write the double entry first, then ask whether either account is income or an expense; if neither is, profit is unaffected. Check whether the item sits in the trading section (gross profit) or only below it, and for an error compare the profit now with what it should be.
