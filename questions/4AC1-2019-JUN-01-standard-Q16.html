  4. Part (d) · 4 marks · Prepare · Provision for depreciation account · A-Grade Plan · practice
    Read part (d) as text

    (d) Prepare the provision for depreciation – motor vehicles account for the year ended 31 March 2019. Balance the account on that date and bring the balance down on 1 April 2019. (4)

    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (d)

    This account normally has a credit balance: opening balance b/d and the year's charge go on the credit side, depreciation on assets sold goes on the debit side as 'Disposal'. Use 'Income statement' as the detail for the year's charge, follow the time policy, and bring the balance down on the first day of next year.

    Hint for part (e)

    In the disposal account, debit the asset's cost and credit both the depreciation on it and the proceeds; the balancing figure is the profit or loss, detail 'Income statement'. Work out the sold asset's depreciation using the time policy, and in any cost account remove only the cost, not the carrying value.
