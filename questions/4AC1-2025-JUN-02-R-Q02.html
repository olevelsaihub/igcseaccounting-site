    Automatic text extract; the original image above is the authority.

    Hint for part (b)

    Work out each partner's closing current account balance first. Show non-current assets at carrying amount, trade receivables net of the allowance, equity in columns per partner (capital and current), and the partner's loan as a liability; deduct any debit current balance.
