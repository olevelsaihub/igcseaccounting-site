Knowledge card

Why businesses give trade discount and cash discount

Written

Trade discount rewards trade and bulk buyers, so it is about the size of the order and is never entered in the ledger. Cash discount rewards credit customers who pay early, so it is about the timing of payment.

  • Trade discount is a reduction from the list price given to trade customers (other businesses) and to customers who buy in bulk.
  • Trade discount is taken off at the time of sale on the invoice and is never recorded in the ledger; only the net amount is entered.
  • Cash discount (settlement discount) is a reduction offered to credit customers if they pay within an agreed time, for example 5% if paid within 30 days.
  • Cash discount is only taken if payment is made inside the time limit; it has nothing to do with paying in notes and coins.
  • Trade discount helps the seller win larger orders, keep customers coming back and grow sales.
  • Cash discount helps the seller get cash in sooner, improving liquidity, and lowers the risk that the debt becomes irrecoverable.

The method

  1. Which discount?

    Check whether the question names trade or cash discount.

  2. Trade: think orders

    Trade discount is about the size of orders and keeping trade customers.

  3. Cash: think timing

    Cash discount is about faster payment and fewer irrecoverable debts.

  4. One point per mark

    Write each benefit as a different short point.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong discount

    A cash discount reason for a trade discount question scores nothing.

  • ✗
    "Cash" means early

    Cash discount is for paying early, not paying in notes and coins.

  • ✗
    Same point twice

    "More sales" and "sells more goods" are one idea.

  • ✗
    Ledger entry

    Trade discount is never recorded in the ledger as an expense.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. The discount named is trade discount, so think about who gets it: trade and bulk buyers.
  2. Benefit 1: a lower price on big orders makes customers order larger quantities at once.
  3. Benefit 2: a good price makes customers want to come back, so they stay loyal and the business keeps their trade.
  4. Check the two points are different ideas, not one idea said twice.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

1 It persuades customers to buy in larger quantities (bulk orders). 2 It keeps customers loyal, so they return and place repeat orders.

How the marks are earned

One mark for each different benefit, up to two. Other sensible benefits, such as helping total sales to rise, are also accepted.
