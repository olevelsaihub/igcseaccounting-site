Evaluating profitability using ratios

Written

To evaluate profitability, say how each ratio moved (with its figures), give a likely reason for the change, and finish with an overall judgement.

What do the profitability ratios tell you?

3 clues to find

Find the three movements and what each one suggests.

  1. Gross profit % steady
  2. Margin fell 5 points
  3. ROCE fell

Gross profit % steady

Movement
It stayed at 32%.
Suggests
Selling prices and the cost of buying goods moved together, so trading is no less profitable.

Margin fell 5 points

Movement
It fell from 14% to 9%.
Suggests
With gross profit % steady, expenses must have grown faster than sales.

Exam tipGive the reason. A movement with no reason earns only half the marks for that ratio.

ROCE fell

Movement
It fell from 11% to 8%.
Suggests
The capital in the business is earning less profit than before.

4 clues to find

The ratios move in opposite directions. Find the four clues you must weigh up.

  1. Gross profit % up
  2. Margin up
  3. ROCE down
  4. Extra capital

Gross profit % up

Movement
It rose from 25% to 30%.
Suggests
Higher selling prices, or cheaper supplies.

Margin up

Movement
It rose from 10% to 12%.
Suggests
More of each $1 of sales is kept as profit.

ROCE down

Movement
It fell from 15% to 13%.
Suggests
Profit has not risen as fast as the capital invested.

Extra capital

Why it matters
Capital rose by $40 000, so ROCE can fall even though profit rose.
Judgement
Margins improved, but the return on capital, the overall measure for the owner, fell.

Exam tipA lower ROCE does not always mean less profit. It can mean more capital.

Does it score?

Exam question

Nadia's profit for the year as a % of revenue fell from 14% to 9%, but her gross profit % stayed at 32%. Evaluate the change in profitability. (3 marks)

Decide first: would this answer earn the mark? Then tap it to check.

Tap an answer to see if it earns the mark.

4

The method

  1. State each movement

    Say whether each ratio rose, fell or stayed the same, with both figures.

  2. Give a reason

    Link each to a cause: prices, cost of sales, expenses or capital.

  3. Weigh them up

    If ratios conflict, decide which matters more.

  4. Give a judgement

    Give a clear judgement: improved, worsened, or right or wrong.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Numbers only

    Figures without a reason lose the development marks.

  • ✗
    Liquidity ratios

    Keep the current and liquid ratios out of a profitability answer.

  • ✗
    ROCE means profit

    ROCE can change because capital changed, not just profit.

  • ✗
    Mixing up the margins

    Gross profit % is about prices; profit % also reflects expenses.

What a complete response includes

Marks are earned in pairs for each ratio: one for correctly saying how it moved and one for a sensible reason or consequence. A final mark is given for an overall judgement linked to the question. Other sensible reasons are accepted.

Worked explanation

  1. Read the table: profit for the year as a % of revenue went from 15% in 2021 to 18% in 2022; ROCE went from 7% to 5%.
  2. Profit margin up 3 percentage points: each $1 of sales now leaves more profit, which suggests expenses are being kept down.
  3. ROCE down 2 percentage points: the capital in the business is earning less profit, so resources are being used less well (perhaps extra capital was invested).
  4. Weigh them: ROCE is the overall measure of return to the owner and it has fallen.
  5. Conclude: overall, profitability has worsened.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Profit for the year as a % of revenue rose from 15% to 18%. This suggests the business has controlled its expenses better, so more of each sale is kept as profit. However, return on capital employed fell from 7% to 5%. The capital invested is producing less profit than before, which shows the business's resources are being used less efficiently. Conclusion: although the profit margin improved, the return on capital fell, so overall profitability worsened from 2021 to 2022.

How the marks are earned

Two marks for the profit margin (its rise and the link to cost control), two for ROCE (its fall and the less efficient use of resources) and one for an overall conclusion. Other sensible reasons are accepted.

Calculation check

  • 18 - 15 = 3
  • 7 - 5 = 2
