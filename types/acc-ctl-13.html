Preparing a bank reconciliation statement

Reconciling the updated cash book balance to the bank statement balance using timing differences.

Type 9 4 Control processes ACC-CTL-13

★★★★☆ · 11 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Difficult.

You are told to prepare a bank reconciliation statement at a date, usually after updating the cash book, with unpresented cheques and outstanding lodgements listed.

Knowledge card

Preparing a bank reconciliation statement

Layout

Start from the updated cash book balance, add unpresented cheques and deduct outstanding lodgements. The answer must equal the bank statement balance, which proves the two records agree.

1

From cash book to bank statement

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

4 features to find

Find why each line is added or deducted, and check the final figure.

  1. Updated balance first
  2. Add unpresented cheques
  3. Deduct lodgements
  4. It must match

Updated balance first

Why
Bank-only items such as charges were entered in the cash book first. Only timing differences are left.
Watch
Starting from the old balance of 2 360 would give the wrong answer.

Add unpresented cheques

Why
The bank has not yet paid Mo, so the bank still holds this money. Its balance is higher.

Deduct lodgements

Why
The bank has not yet added the $700 takings, so its balance is lower.
Layout
Brackets show the amount is taken off.

It must match

Check
2 210 + 560 − 700 = 2 070, the figure on the bank statement.
Label
Write Balance per bank statement, not just the number.
0 of 4 found

3 features to find

Follow the signs when the starting balance is negative.

  1. Overdraft in brackets
  2. Same rules apply
  3. Shown as Dr by the bank

Overdraft in brackets

Why
An overdraft is a negative balance. Keep the brackets so the sign is not lost.

Same rules apply

Working
−1 340 + 820 = −520. The account is still overdrawn.

Exam tipAdding 820 to an overdraft makes it smaller, not bigger.

Shown as Dr by the bank

Working
−520 − 300 = −820.
Meaning
The bank statement shows $820 Dr, an overdraft.
0 of 3 found
2

The calculation

Bank statement balance = Updated cash book balance + Unpresented cheques − Outstanding lodgements

Overdrafts are negative numbers. The same signs apply.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find BS:BS = CB + UC − OL

To find CB:CB = BS − UC + OL

Try itUpdated cash book $2 210; unpresented cheque $560; lodgement $700. Bank statement balance?

  1. Start with 2 210.
  2. Add the unpresented cheque: 2 210 + 560 = 2 770.
  3. Deduct the lodgement: 2 770 − 700 = 2 070.
  4. Bank statement balance: $2 070 (Cr).

5

The method

  1. Heading and start

    Write the heading and date. Start with the updated cash book balance.

  2. Adjust for timing

    Add unpresented cheques; deduct outstanding lodgements.

  3. Label the result

    Write Balance per bank statement next to the final figure.

  4. Check it

    It must equal the statement balance: Cr is positive, Dr is an overdraft.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Old cash book balance

    Always start from the updated balance.

  • ✗
    Signs reversed

    From the cash book: add unpresented cheques, deduct outstanding lodgements.

  • ✗
    Bank-only items included

    Standing orders and bank charges go in the cash book, not here.

  • ✗
    Lost overdraft sign

    Keep the brackets on a negative balance.

What a complete response includes

One mark for the opening balance (often own-figure from the updated cash book), one each for the unpresented cheques and outstanding lodgements with labels, and one for the final bank statement balance. The reverse layout, starting from the bank statement, earns the same marks.

Worked explanation

  1. Start from the updated cash book balance found in part (a): 1 028 (debit, money in the bank).
  2. Unpresented cheque to Fatima, 248: the bank has not yet paid it, so the bank balance is higher - add 248.
  3. Outstanding lodgement of cash sales, 890: the bank has not yet credited it, so the bank balance is lower - deduct 890.
  4. 1 028 + 248 - 890 = 386.
  5. This equals the bank statement credit balance of $386, so the statement reconciles.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Nabil Bank Reconciliation Statement at 30 April 2024 | $ Balance as per updated cash book | 1 028 Add: Unpresented cheque (Fatima) | 248 Less: Outstanding lodgement (cash sales) | (890) Balance per bank statement | 386

How the marks are earned

One own-figure mark for the updated cash book balance of 1 028, one for adding the unpresented cheque of 248, one for deducting the outstanding lodgement of 890, and one for the bank statement balance of 386. The reverse layout (bank statement 386, less 248, add 890, giving 1 028) is equally acceptable.

Calculation check

  • 1028 + 248 - 890 = 386
  • 386 - 248 + 890 = 1028
