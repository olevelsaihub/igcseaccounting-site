Knowledge card

Statement of financial position from incomplete records

Layout

A sole trader's statement of financial position from incomplete records follows the usual order, but equity starts from opening equity, adds additional capital and profit, and deducts drawings.

1

Build the statement from what you know

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

4 features to find

Find where each equity figure comes from.

  1. Opening equity
  2. Asset from own money
  3. Profit as balancing figure
  4. Cash and goods

Opening equity

Working
Assets 2 000 + 20 000 + 11 000 = 33 000; less trade payables 3 000 = 30 000.

Asset from own money

Why it matters
Ola paid 6 000 of her own money for new equipment: additional capital and a new asset.

Profit as balancing figure

Working
Total equity 39 800 - 30 000 - 6 000 + 11 600 = 15 400.
Why it matters
Total equity = total assets - total liabilities.

Cash and goods

Working
Cash 11 000 + goods taken for own use 600 = 11 600.

3 features to find

Find how the non-current asset and current assets are worked out.

  1. Both items depreciated
  2. Cash is an asset
  3. Total assets

Both items depreciated

Working
Cost 20 000 + 6 000 = 26 000; 26 000 x 10% = 2 600; carrying amount 23 400.

Cash is an asset

Why it matters
Cash in hand is a current asset.

Total assets

Check
18 300 + 9 100 + 400 = 27 800; 23 400 + 27 800 = 51 200.

4 features to find

Find how each loan is classified and check the statement balances.

  1. Non-current loan
  2. Current loan
  3. Overdraft: current
  4. It balances

Non-current loan

Why it matters
It is repayable more than 12 months after the statement date.

Current loan

Why it matters
It is repayable within 12 months, so it is a current liability.

Overdraft: current

Why it matters
A bank overdraft is a current liability, never an asset.

It balances

Check
1 500 + 4 600 + 1 300 = 7 400; 4 000 + 7 400 = 11 400; 39 800 + 11 400 = 51 200 = total assets.

2

The equity section

Closing equity = Opening equity + Additional capital + Profit − Drawings

If profit is unknown: Profit = Closing equity − Opening equity − Additional capital + Drawings.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find CE:CE = OE + AC + P − D

Try itAssets $51 200; liabilities $11 400; opening equity $30 000; additional capital $6 000; drawings $11 600. Find profit.

  1. Total equity: 51 200 - 11 400 = 39 800.
  2. Less opening equity: 9 800.
  3. Less capital: 3 800.
  4. Add drawings: $15 400 profit.

5

The method

  1. Opening equity

    Opening assets less opening liabilities.

  2. Assets and loans

    Assets at carrying amount; loans due within a year are current.

  3. Build equity

    Opening equity + additional capital + profit - drawings (cash and goods).

  4. Balance it

    If profit is unknown, find it so total equity and liabilities equals total assets.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Missing goods taken

    Goods taken for own use are drawings.

  • ✗
    Missing the private-money asset

    Show it as an asset and as additional capital.

  • ✗
    Short loan as non-current

    A loan due within a year is a current liability.

  • ✗
    Depreciating one asset only

    Depreciate the new asset as well as the old one.

(ii) Prepare the statement of financial position at 31 December 2023. (15)

Find opening equity from the opening assets and liabilities, then build equity: add new capital and profit, deduct drawings (including goods taken). Depreciate every asset held, classify each loan by when it is repaid, and show the overdraft as a current liability.

Worked explanation

  1. Opening equity (part a.i): cash 5 000 + motor vehicle 12 000 - bank loan 5 000 = $12 000.
  2. Motor vehicles: cost 12 000 + 10 000 = $22 000; depreciation 22 000 x 20% = $4 400; carrying amount $17 600.
  3. Current assets: add inventory of 14 568, trade receivables of 2 167, other receivables of 200 and cash of 890, giving $17 825; total assets 17 600 + 17 825 = $35 425.
  4. Non-current liability: bank loan repayable 2028, $5 000.
  5. Current liabilities: bank loan repayable June 2024 $3 000 + overdraft 2 198 + trade payables 2 325 + other payables 567 = $8 090; total liabilities $13 090.
  6. Total equity must be 35 425 - 13 090 = $22 335.
  7. Additional capital is the $10 000 vehicle paid from her own money; drawings are 4 000 cash + 800 goods = $4 800.
  8. Profit (balancing figure): 22 335 - 12 000 - 10 000 + 4 800 = $5 135.

How the marks are earned

Marks go to the motor vehicles figure, the current assets, the current asset total, total assets, opening equity, additional capital, profit, drawings, total equity, the long-term loan, the short-term loan, the overdraft, other payables, the current liabilities total and the final total. Several totals are marked on own figures.
