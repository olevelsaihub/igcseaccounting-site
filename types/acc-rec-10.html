Knowledge card

Preparing an analysed petty cash book

Layout

In an analysed petty cash book each payment goes in twice: the total column and its analysis column. The top-up from the bank restores the float at month end.

1

Inside a petty cash book

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

4 features to find

Check that each payment is entered twice and that the columns add across.

  1. Entered twice
  2. Date and details
  3. Total spent
  4. Columns add across

Entered twice

What to see
$14 is in the total column and again under Postage.
Why it matters
Each line earns its mark only with date, detail and both amounts.

Date and details

Why
Dates and details are marked together with the amount.
Write it as
Mar 15, Parcel post, $23.

Total spent

Working
14 + 9 + 23 + 18 + 11 = $75.
Next step
This is the amount to restore from the bank.

Columns add across

Working
Postage 14 + 23 + 11 = $48; Travel 9 + 18 = $27.
Check
$48 + $27 = $75, the total column.

Exam tipIf the analysis totals do not add to the total, find the error before you balance.

5 features to find

Find the opening balance, the restoring receipt and the balance brought down.

  1. Opening balance
  2. Restoring receipt
  3. Balance c/d = float
  4. Equal totals
  5. Balance b/d next month

Opening balance

Where
In the receipts column, dated, with Balance b/d.
Meaning
The float in the box at the start of the month.

Restoring receipt

Working
Restore exactly what was spent: $75.
Where
Receipts column, details Bank, on the last day.

Exam tipThe top-up is a receipt for the petty cash book, so it never goes on the payments side.

Balance c/d = float

Working
After the top-up the box holds $200 again.
Careful
Not the $125 left before the top-up.

Equal totals

Working
Receipts 200 + 75 = $275. Payments 75 + 200 = $275.
Why
Both sides must total the same.

Balance b/d next month

Where
Receipts column, dated the first day of the next month.
Write it as
Apr 1, Balance b/d, $200.

4

The method

  1. Opening balance

    Enter the float in the receipts column with the date and Balance b/d.

  2. Each payment

    Date, details, total, and the same amount in its analysis column.

  3. Total and cross-check

    Total every column and check the analysis totals add to the total column.

  4. Restore the float

    Enter amount spent as a receipt from Bank; balance c/d is the float.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong analysis column

    Read headings; stamps go under Postage if it has a column.

  • ✗
    Top-up on the wrong side

    The money from the bank is a receipt, not a payment.

  • ✗
    Missing dates or details

    Each line is marked with its date, detail and amount together.

  • ✗
    Wrong balance c/d

    After restoring, the balance c/d is the full float, not the cash left before the top-up.

What a complete response includes

Marks go for each correctly dated, described and analysed payment, then for the restoring receipt, the balance c/d and the balance b/d. Dates, details and amounts are marked together.

Worked explanation

  1. Opening balance 1 January: $250 in receipts.
  2. Analysis columns given: Postage and stationery, Travel, Cleaning.
  3. Jan 2 Stamps $12 to Postage and stationery. Jan 5 Taxi fare $6 to Travel. Jan 10 Stationery $21 to Postage and stationery. Jan 18 Office cleaner $30 to Cleaning. Jan 24 Printer ink $15 to Postage and stationery. Jan 28 Train fare $26 to Travel.
  4. Total spent = 12 + 6 + 21 + 30 + 15 + 26 = $110.
  5. Column totals: Postage and stationery 12 + 21 + 15 = $48; Travel 6 + 26 = $32; Cleaning $30. 48 + 32 + 30 = 110.
  6. Jan 31: restore $110 from the bank in receipts.
  7. Receipts total 250 + 110 = $360. Payments: 110 + balance c/d 250 = $360.
  8. 1 February: balance b/d $250.

How the marks are earned

One mark for the opening balance, one for each of the six payment lines (date, detail, total and analysis together), one for the $110 from the bank, and one for the $250 balance carried and brought down.

Calculation check

  • 12 + 6 + 21 + 30 + 15 + 26 = 110
  • 12 + 21 + 15 = 48
  • 6 + 26 = 32
  • 48 + 32 + 30 = 110
  • 250 + 110 = 360
  • 360 - 110 = 250
