Knowledge card

Calculating the provision for irrecoverable debts

Calculation

Take any write-off away from trade receivables first, then apply the rate to find the new provision. Only the change from last year's provision goes to the income statement, as an increase or a decrease.

1

Work out the provision

Follow the steps to the change in the provision.

  1. Write-off first
  2. New provision
  3. Increase: an expense

Write-off first

Why
The $400 will never be collected, so it is not part of the base.
Working
$25 400 − $400 = $25 000

New provision

Working
4% × $25 000 = $1 000
Meaning
The closing balance of the provision account.

Increase: an expense

Working
$1 000 − $850 = $150
Effect
$150 is an expense, so profit falls by $150.

Exam tipCharge the $150 change, not the whole $1 000.

Decide whether the change is an increase or a decrease.

  1. New provision
  2. Old is bigger
  3. Decrease: added to profit

New provision

Working
5% × $18 000 = $900

Old is bigger

Why
The opening $1 100 is more than the $900 needed.

Decrease: added to profit

Working
$1 100 − $900 = $200
Effect
A decrease is added to profit for the year.

Exam tipAlways write the word "increase" or "decrease" with the figure.

2 figures to find

Work out the rate of the provision.

  1. Provision ÷ receivables
  2. Base: after write-offs

Provision ÷ receivables

Working
$960 ÷ $32 000 × 100 = 3%

Base: after write-offs

Why
Rates are always applied to receivables once known losses are removed.

2

The calculation

Provision = Rate % × (Trade receivables − debts written off)

Change = new − opening provision. Positive: expense. Negative: adds to profit.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find P new:r × (TR − W)

To find Change:P new − P old

To find r:P new ÷ (TR − W) × 100

Try itReceivables $42 600 incl. $600 to write off; provision 5%; opening provision $1 800. Find the change.

  1. Receivables: $42 600 − $600 = $42 000
  2. New provision: 5% × $42 000 = $2 100
  3. Change = $2 100 − $1 800 = $300
  4. Bigger: an increase of $300 (expense).

5

The method

  1. Remove write-offs

    Subtract any irrecoverable debt from trade receivables.

  2. New provision

    Multiply what is left by the rate.

  3. Compare

    Subtract the smaller from the larger of the new and opening provision.

  4. Name the direction

    Write increase (an expense) or decrease (added to profit).

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Write-off left in

    Apply the rate after taking off the debt written off.

  • ✗
    New figure, not change

    Read whether the question wants the provision or the change.

  • ✗
    No direction

    A figure without "increase" or "decrease" loses a mark.

  • ✗
    Direction reversed

    If the new provision is smaller, it is a decrease.

What a complete response includes

One mark for each correct figure (adjusted receivables, new provision, change) and one mark for saying increase or decrease. A partly correct change (for example using unadjusted receivables) can still earn some credit.

(b) Calculate the change in the provision for irrecoverable debts at 31 March 2024 stating clearly whether the change is an increase or decrease in the provision. (3)

Hint for part (b)

Figures check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Trade receivables at 31 March 2024: $28 750, including a $450 irrecoverable debt.
  2. Adjusted receivables: $28 750 - $450 = $28 300.
  3. New provision at 4%: 4% x $28 300 = $1 132.
  4. Opening provision: $1 205.
  5. Change: $1 205 - $1 132 = $73. The new figure is smaller, so it is a decrease.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Trade receivables after write-off = $28 750 - $450 = $28 300 Provision required = 4% x $28 300 = $1 132 Change = $1 205 - $1 132 = $73 The provision has decreased by $73.

How the marks are earned

Two marks for the correct change of $73 and one for stating it is a decrease. If the write-off is forgotten (4% x $28 750 = $1 150, change $55), one mark is still given for the figure and one for 'decrease'.

Calculation check

  • 28750 - 450 = 28300
  • 28300 × 4 ÷ 100 = 1132
  • 1205 - 1132 = 73
  • 28750 × 4 ÷ 100 = 1150
  • 1205 - 1150 = 55
