Knowledge card

Updating the cash book from the bank statement

Layout

Before reconciling, update the cash book with items only on the bank statement: money in on the debit side, money out on the credit side. Items already in the cash book, such as unpresented cheques, are left out.

1

Bringing the cash book up to date

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

4 entries to find

Find which items go into the cash book and which are left out.

  1. Money in: debit
  2. Dishonoured: credit
  3. The difference only
  4. Leave timing items out

Money in: debit

Why
Hana paid straight into the bank, so the bank balance is higher.
Detail
Name the payer: Hana.

Dishonoured: credit

Why
Nadia recorded Joe's $140 as received, but the money never arrived. Crediting the cash book takes it out again.

Exam tipDebiting a dishonoured cheque is a common slip. The money has gone, so it is a credit.

The difference only

Why 27
$247 is already in the cash book. The payment should be $274, so 274 − 247 = 27 more goes on the credit side.

Leave timing items out

Why
The cheque to Mo and the takings are already in the cash book. Only the bank has not caught up.
Where they go
In the bank reconciliation statement.

4 entries to find

Check each feature of the updated bank column.

  1. Opening on the debit side
  2. Money out: credit
  3. Equal totals
  4. Balance brought down

Opening on the debit side

Why
A debit balance in the cash book means money in the bank.
Watch
An overdraft would start on the credit side.

Money out: credit

Why
Direct debits, standing orders, bank charges and dishonoured cheques all reduce the bank balance.
Detail
Name the expense or the person.

Equal totals

Debit
2 360 + 215 + 12 = 2 587.
Credit
180 + 140 + 30 + 27 = 377, so the balance c/d is 2 587 − 377 = 2 210.

Balance brought down

Write it as
1 November 2025, Balance b/d, 2 210 on the debit side.
Why it matters
This updated figure is where the reconciliation starts.

2

The calculation

Updated balance = Cash book balance + Money in shown by the bank − Money out shown by the bank

Use only bank statement items not yet in cash book.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find CB:CB = Updated balance − In + Out

  • An overdraft is a negative starting figure.
  • Correct errors by the difference only.

Try itNadia's cash book shows $2 360 in the bank. Find the updated balance.

  1. Money in: 215 + 12 = 227.
  2. Money out: 180 + 140 + 30 + 27 = 377.
  3. 2 360 + 227 − 377 = 2 210.
  4. Updated balance: $2 210 (debit).

5

The method

  1. Opening balance

    Enter it as Balance b/d: debit if in the bank, credit if overdrawn.

  2. Sort and post the items

    Only bank statement items go in. Money in: debit; money out: credit.

  3. Fix errors

    Correct a cash book error by the difference only.

  4. Balance it

    Total both sides, enter Balance c/d, then bring it down on the next day.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Timing items entered

    Unpresented cheques and outstanding lodgements are never entered here.

  • ✗
    Dishonoured cheque debited

    The money did not arrive, so credit the cash book.

  • ✗
    Overdraft on the debit side

    A cash book overdraft is a credit balance.

  • ✗
    Full amount for an error

    The wrong figure is already there. Post only the difference.

Hint for part (a)

Worked explanation

  1. Opening cash book balance: debit 1 410 (money in the bank) - enter as Balance b/d on the debit side.
  2. Items only on the bank statement go into the cash book: debit side - bank interest received 28, electronic transfer from Bob 85; credit side - standing order for rent 350, Petra's dishonoured cheque 110, bank charges 35.
  3. Items only in the cash book (cheque to Fatima 248, cash sales banked 890) are left out; they belong in the reconciliation.
  4. Debit total: 1 410 + 28 + 85 = 1 523.
  5. Credit items: 350 + 110 + 35 = 495. Balance c/d: 1 523 - 495 = 1 028.
  6. Bring down 1 028 on the debit side on 1 May 2024.

Cash Book - Bank Column Only Debit side: 2024 Apr 30 | Balance b/d | 1 410 Apr 30 | Bank interest | 28 Apr 30 | Bob | 85 Total | 1 523 May 1 | Balance b/d | 1 028

Credit side: 2024 Apr 30 | Rent | 350 Apr 30 | Petra (dishonoured cheque) | 110 Apr 30 | Bank charges | 35 Apr 30 | Balance c/d | 1 028 Total | 1 523

How the marks are earned

One mark for each of the five new items with date, detail and amount on the right side (rent 350, interest 28, Petra 110, Bob 85, bank charges 35), one mark for the opening balance, and one own-figure mark for the balance of 1 028 brought down on 1 May.

Calculation check

  • 1410 + 28 + 85 = 1523
  • 350 + 110 + 35 = 495
  • 1523 - 495 = 1028
