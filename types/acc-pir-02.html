Partnership agreements and what happens without one

Written

Without an agreement, the Partnership Act 1890 applies: profits are shared equally, with no salaries or interest on capital or drawings, and 5% on partners' loans.

4 rules to find

Find the four items a partnership agreement usually settles.

  1. Profit-sharing ratio
  2. Interest on capital
  3. Partner's salary
  4. Interest on loans

Profit-sharing ratio

Why
Sets how the remaining profit is split.
Without it
Profits would be shared equally.

Interest on capital

Why
Rewards the partner who has put in more capital.
Without it
No interest on capital is allowed.

Partner's salary

Why
Rewards a partner who does more of the work.
Without it
No partner gets a salary.

Interest on loans

Why
A partner may lend extra money to the business.
Without it
The Act gives 5% a year on the loan.

3 rules to find

Find the three rules that students most often get wrong.

  1. Shared equally
  2. No interest on capital
  3. 5% on loans only

Shared equally

Rule
Profits and losses are split equally.
Not
Not in the ratio of capital.

No interest on capital

Rule
No interest is paid on capital.
Not
The 5% is not on capital.

5% on loans only

Rule
A partner's loan beyond capital earns 5% a year.
Example
A loan of $10 000 earns 10 000 x 5% = $500.

Find the three figures that show the Act's rules at work.

  1. Unequal capital
  2. 5% on Bea's loan
  3. Equal shares

Unequal capital

Point
Amir has put in three times as much capital as Bea.
But
He gets no interest on it.

5% on Bea's loan

Work out
10 000 x 5% = 500.

Equal shares

Work out
30 000 / 2 = 15 000 each.
Why it matters
Amir may feel this is unfair, which is why an agreement helps.

The method

  1. Recall the Act

    Equal shares, no salary, no interest on capital or drawings, 5% on loans.

  2. Purpose and effect

    Say what the agreement does, as evidence of terms, and its effect: fewer disputes.

  3. Without one

    Explain the Act's rules and why they may be unfair.

  4. Give a recommendation

    For an advise question, end with a clear recommendation.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    5% on capital

    The Act's 5% is only on partners' loans.

  • ✗
    Ratio of capital

    The Act shares profits equally.

  • ✗
    Salaries under the Act

    There are no partners' salaries without an agreement.

  • ✗
    No recommendation

    Advise questions keep a mark for the decision.

Worked explanation

  1. Haitai and Ming formed a partnership but wrote no agreement.
  2. Point 1: an agreement sets out each partner's rights and duties, so they can check it if they disagree.
  3. Point 2: it prevents arguments that could slow down business decisions.
  4. Point 3: without it, the Partnership Act 1890 applies, so profits and losses are shared equally whatever each partner puts in.
  5. Decision: recommend writing an agreement.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

A formal agreement would set out clearly what each partner is entitled to and responsible for, so if Haitai and Ming disagree they can look at the document to settle it. Having the terms written down should stop arguments starting, and fewer arguments means decisions about the business can be made more quickly. Without an agreement the Partnership Act 1890 will apply. This means profits and losses are split equally and neither partner can have a salary or interest on capital, even if one partner works more or invests more. I advise Haitai and Ming to draw up a formal partnership agreement.

How the marks are earned

Up to four marks are for explaining why an agreement is useful: knowing rights and responsibilities, being able to refer to it in a dispute, avoiding disputes that slow decisions, and the Act's equal sharing applying if there is no agreement. One mark is for the recommendation. Other reasonable points are accepted.
