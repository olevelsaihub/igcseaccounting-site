Why keep a full set of accounting records

Written

A full set of accounting records means every transaction is recorded by double entry, giving accurate, up-to-date figures for statements, decisions and control, at the cost of time and money.

5 benefits to find

Find the benefits of full records, and the one drawback.

  1. Statements more often
  2. Better decisions
  3. Errors found
  4. Trusted by others
  5. The drawback

Statements more often

Identify
Financial statements can be prepared quickly and as often as needed.
Develop
The figures are already in the books, so nothing has to be pieced together.

Better decisions

Identify
The owner can make better decisions.
Develop
The information used is complete, accurate and current, not estimated.

Errors found

Identify
A trial balance can be drawn up.
Develop
This helps to find some errors in the records.

Trusted by others

Identify
Banks and tax authorities trust complete records.
Develop
So the business finds it easier to borrow money.

The drawback

Why it matters
Keeping full records takes time and may cost money, for example paying a bookkeeper.

Exam tipIn an evaluate answer, weigh this drawback before you give your judgement.

3 benefits to find

Find how full records help the owner control trade receivables.

  1. Who to chase
  2. Total known at once
  3. Each customer's account

Who to chase

Identify
The owner can see which customers are slow to pay.
Develop
So they can be chased, which reduces the risk of irrecoverable debts.

Total known at once

Why it matters
1 200 + 3 400 + 600 = 5 200. The owner knows exactly what is owed without rebuilding it.

Each customer's account

Why it matters
Each credit customer has an account, so any dispute can be checked against the record.

The method

  1. Count the reasons

    Give exactly the number of reasons asked for, usually two.

  2. Identify each one

    State each benefit clearly in one phrase.

  3. Develop each one

    Add because or so that to show the effect on the business or owner.

  4. Evaluate if asked

    Weigh a drawback, such as time or cost, then give an overall judgement.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    A bare list

    Develop each reason, or half the marks go.

  • ✗
    Same reason twice

    Each reason must be a different benefit, not the same one reworded.

  • ✗
    Too many reasons

    Only the number asked for is credited, so develop two well.

  • ✗
    No conclusion

    In an evaluate answer, finish with a clear judgement.

Worked explanation

  1. Reason 1: statements can be prepared more often. Develop: the figures are already recorded, so nothing needs to be rebuilt.
  2. Reason 2: better decisions. Develop: the owner relies on figures that are complete, accurate and current.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

First, with full records Thelma could produce financial statements whenever she needs them, for example every month, because all the figures are already recorded in the books and do not have to be pieced together. Second, full records help the owner make better decisions, since the information she uses is accurate and up to date rather than estimated.

How the marks are earned

Each reason earns one mark for being identified and one for its development, two reasons for four marks. Other valid reasons, such as better control of receivables and payables or easier access to bank loans, are also accepted.
