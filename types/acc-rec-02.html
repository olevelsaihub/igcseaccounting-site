Matching each transaction to its source document (and book)

Written

A source document is the original evidence of a transaction. Identify the type (sale, purchase, return, money) and credit or cash/bank; that points to the document and book of original entry.

4

The method

  1. What kind?

    Is it a sale, a purchase, a return, a receipt of money or a payment?

  2. Credit or money?

    Credit: invoices, credit notes. Money: receipts, counterfoils, bank statement.

  3. Our point of view

    Name the document the business holds, e.g. the supplier's credit note for a return.

  4. Match the book

    Day books for credit items, cash book for bank and cash, journal for the rest.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Cash or credit

    An invoice is for credit; a cash sale has a receipt.

  • ✗
    Asset on credit

    A non-current asset goes in the journal, not the purchases day book.

  • ✗
    Wrong side

    A sales invoice for a purchase is the seller's view, not ours.

  • ✗
    Credit transfer

    Its evidence is the bank statement or a remittance advice, not a receipt.

Worked explanation

  1. Receipt from a customer by credit transfer: the money goes straight into the bank. The customer sends a remittance advice, and the bank statement shows the receipt.
  2. Return of goods to a credit supplier: the supplier accepts the goods back and sends a credit note.
  3. Purchase of a non-current asset on credit: the supplier sends an invoice, which to us is a purchase invoice.
  4. Receipt of bank interest: only the bank knows the amount, so the evidence is the bank statement.
  5. Issue of a cheque for personal use: the business keeps the cheque counterfoil as its record of the cheque written.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Transaction | Source document Sale of goods to a credit customer | Sales invoice (given) Receipt from a customer by credit transfer | Remittance advice (or bank statement) Return of goods to a credit supplier | Credit note Purchase of a non-current asset on credit | Purchase invoice Receipt of bank interest | Bank statement Issue of a cheque for personal use | Cheque counterfoil

How the marks are earned

One mark for each of the five blank rows. For the credit transfer, either the remittance advice or the bank statement is accepted.
