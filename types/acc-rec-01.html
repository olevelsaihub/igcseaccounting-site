Knowledge card

Business documents: what each one is and why it is sent

Written

Every business document has a fixed sender, a fixed receiver and a job. Ask who sends it, what it records and when it is sent, and you can name any document.

1

Which document is this?

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

4 documents to find

Find who sends each document and why it is sent.

  1. Buyer asks for goods
  2. One credit sale
  3. Sent by the seller
  4. Sent with a payment

Buyer asks for goods

Sent by
The buyer, before any sale happens.
In the books?
No: there is no sale yet, so nothing is recorded.

One credit sale

Sent by
The seller to a credit customer.
Shows
Goods, price, any trade discount and the amount owed.

Sent by the seller

When
Goods are returned, or the customer was overcharged.
Effect
It reduces what the customer owes.

Exam tipA debit note is raised by the buyer to ask for a credit. Do not mix it up with a credit note.

Sent with a payment

Sent by
The customer, with or before a payment.
Not
A receipt, which is proof that cash was paid.

4 documents to find

Find the features that show this is a statement of account, not an invoice.

  1. Opening balance
  2. Returns included
  3. Payments included
  4. Balance still owed

Opening balance

Why
A statement starts from what was owed at the start of the period.
Invoice?
An invoice shows one sale only, with no opening balance.

Returns included

Why
The statement brings returns into the summary.
Check
1 200 + 850 − 150 − 1 000 = 900

Payments included

Why
Payments received during the month are listed too.
Key idea
Only the statement covers a whole period.

Balance still owed

Why
It ends with the amount the customer still owes.
Sent
Usually at the end of each month.

State the purpose of a credit note. (1 mark)

Decide first: would this answer earn the mark? Then tap it to check.

Tap an answer to see if it earns the mark.

4

The method

  1. Who sends it?

    Seller, buyer, bank or the business itself.

  2. What does it record?

    An order, one credit sale, a return, a month's summary, a payment or bank activity.

  3. When is it sent?

    Before the sale, at the sale, after a return, at month end, or with a payment.

  4. Name it exactly

    Write the standard title, or one specific purpose if a purpose is asked.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Invoice or statement

    An invoice is one sale; a statement summarises a whole period.

  • ✗
    Credit or debit note

    A debit note comes from the buyer to ask for a credit.

  • ✗
    Remittance or receipt

    Remittance goes with a payment; a receipt proves cash was paid.

  • ✗
    Vague purpose

    "To record the transaction" fits every document and scores nothing.

What a complete response includes

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. The question asks for a document that shows sales returns AND payments made during a period.
  2. A credit note shows one return only, so A is wrong.
  3. A debit note is raised by the buyer to ask for a credit; it does not list payments, so B is wrong.
  4. A sales invoice shows one sale only, so C is wrong.
  5. A statement of account summarises a whole period: invoices, returns and payments, ending with the balance due. So the answer is D.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

D Statement of account

How the marks are earned

One mark for choosing D. The key idea is that only the statement of account covers a period and brings together returns and payments.
