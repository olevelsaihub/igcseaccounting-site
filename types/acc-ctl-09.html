Knowledge card

The suspense account

Layout

A suspense account holds the difference when the trial balance totals do not agree. It opens on the short side, takes the suspense leg of each correction, and clears to nil once every error is found.

4

The method

  1. Find the difference

    Take the smaller trial balance total from the larger one.

  2. Open on the short side

    Enter it as Balance b/d on the side whose total was smaller.

  3. Find each suspense leg

    Ignore omission, commission and principle errors; take the suspense leg of the rest.

  4. Post and prove it

    Post each leg on the side the journal shows, then total: they must agree.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Opening on the larger side

    The difference always goes on the side that was short.

  • ✗
    Hidden errors included

    Omission and principle errors never go through suspense.

  • ✗
    Wrong side in suspense

    The suspense entry is the opposite side to the other account in the journal.

  • ✗
    Single amount for a wrong side

    A wrong-side posting needs twice the amount.

What a complete response includes

In written items, one mark per point or for naming the account. In preparation items, one mark for the opening difference and one for each suspense correction with its detail and amount, with own-figure credit for an opening balance that follows from an earlier part.

Worked explanation

  1. Trial balance totals: debit 41 970, credit 42 550. Debit is short by 42 550 - 41 970 = 580, so the suspense account opens with a debit 'Balance b/d' of 580.
  2. Error 1: sales day book overcast by 200. Correction: debit Sales 200, credit Suspense 200. Suspense credit: Sales 200.
  3. Error 2: motor repairs posted to motor vehicles - principle error, no suspense entry.
  4. Error 3: payment to Raul, 450, not posted to Raul's account. Correction: debit Raul 450, credit Suspense 450. Suspense credit: Raul 450.
  5. Error 4: discount received 80 posted to the debit of discount allowed. Correction: credit Discount allowed 80 and credit Discount received 80, debit Suspense 160. Suspense debit: Discount received 80 and Discount allowed 80.
  6. Error 5: cash sales not recorded - omission, no suspense entry.
  7. Error 6: rent 430 posted as 340 in the rent account. Difference 430 - 340 = 90: debit Rent 90, credit Suspense 90. Suspense credit: Rent 90.
  8. Totals: debit 580 + 80 + 80 = 740; credit 200 + 450 + 90 = 740. The account clears.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Suspense Account Debit side: 2019 Apr 30 | Balance b/d | 580 Apr 30 | Discount received | 80 Apr 30 | Discount allowed | 80 Total | 740

Credit side: 2019 Apr 30 | Sales | 200 Apr 30 | Raul | 450 Apr 30 | Rent | 90 Total | 740

How the marks are earned

One mark each for the opening 580, Sales 200, Raul 450, Discount received 80, Discount allowed 80 and Rent 90, each needing the right detail and side together with the date. The totals of 740 confirm the account has cleared. A single debit entry of 160 would not show the two separate accounts the scheme credits.

Calculation check

  • 42550 - 41970 = 580
  • 430 - 340 = 90
  • 580 + 80 + 80 = 740
  • 200 + 450 + 90 = 740
