Knowledge card

Completing an invoice or credit note

Calculation

To complete an invoice, multiply quantity by unit price for each line, add the lines to get the subtotal, then deduct trade discount on the subtotal. Cash discount stays as a term and is not deducted.

5 figures to find

Find how each money figure on the invoice is worked out.

  1. Line total
  2. Subtotal
  3. Trade discount
  4. Total owed
  5. Not deducted

Line total

Working
40 × $35.00 = $1 400
Check
Each line is quantity × unit price.

Subtotal

Working
$1 400 + $1 440 = $2 840
Why
Trade discount is worked out on this figure.

Trade discount

Working
$2 840 × 20% = $568
Not
20% of one line only.

Total owed

Working
$2 840 − $568 = $2 272
Recorded
This net figure goes in the books.

Not deducted

Why
Cash discount is only earned if Lagoon Cafe pays within 30 days.
Do this
Leave it as a term below the total.

Exam tipNever take the cash discount off the invoice total.

Find why the customer is credited $112, not $140.

  1. List price of returns
  2. Same trade discount
  3. Net amount credited

List price of returns

Working
4 × $35.00 = $140
Same as
The invoice layout.

Same trade discount

Working
$140 × 20% = $28
Why
The customer was charged the net price, so is credited the net price.

Net amount credited

Working
$140 − $28 = $112
Trap
Crediting $140 would give back more than was charged.

2

The calculation

Total owed = Subtotal − Trade discount

Subtotal = sum of quantity × unit price; trade discount = subtotal × %.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find Subtotal:Subtotal = Total owed + Trade discount

Try it25 lamps at $8.00 and 60 bulbs at $4.50; trade discount 10%; cash discount 5% within 30 days. Find the invoice total.

  1. Lamps: 25 × $8.00 = $200
  2. Bulbs: 60 × $4.50 = $270
  3. Subtotal: $200 + $270 = $470
  4. Trade discount: $470 × 10% = $47
  5. Total owed: $470 − $47 = $423
  6. Cash discount is not deducted: $423.

5

The method

  1. Line totals

    Multiply quantity × unit price for each line.

  2. Find the subtotal

    Add the line totals to get the subtotal at list price.

  3. Trade discount and total

    Take trade discount off the subtotal to get the total owed.

  4. Leave cash discount

    Keep the cash discount as a term; do not deduct it.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Deducting cash discount

    Only trade discount is taken off on the invoice.

  • ✗
    Discount on one line

    Work out trade discount on the whole subtotal.

  • ✗
    Arithmetic slips

    Check each multiplication; later figures build on it.

  • ✗
    Credit note at list price

    Credit the net amount, after the same trade discount.

What a complete response includes

A mark for each correct line total, then marks for the subtotal, the trade discount and the net total. Later figures are credited on the student's own earlier figures if the method is right.

Worked explanation

  1. (i) 3 core cable: 150 x $5.00 = $750.
  2. (ii) 2 core cable: 400 x $2.20 = $880.
  3. (iii) Subtotal: $750 + $880 = $1 630.
  4. (iv) Trade discount 20%: $1 630 x 20% = $326.
  5. (v) Total: $1 630 - $326 = $1 304.
  6. The 5% for 30 days is a cash discount term, so it is not deducted.

How the marks are earned

One mark for each of the five figures. The subtotal, discount and total are marked on your own figures, so a slip in a line total does not lose every later mark.
