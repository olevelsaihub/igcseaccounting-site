Ratio formulas and how to calculate them

Calculation

Every ratio is a top figure divided by a bottom figure. The three profitability ratios are percentages (x 100), and the two liquidity ratios are written as x : 1.

1

Read the ratios and the figures

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

4 features to find

Find the four parts that most often cost a mark.

  1. x 100 for a percentage
  2. ROCE
  3. Write it as x : 1
  4. Inventory taken out

x 100 for a percentage

Why
The profitability ratios are percentages.
Scores
Leaving out x 100 loses the mark.

ROCE

Meaning
Return on capital employed: profit earned from the capital invested.
Write it as
Profit for the year / capital employed x 100.

Write it as x : 1

Why
Liquidity ratios compare assets with debts.
Scores
Write it as, e.g., 3.30 : 1, not a %.

Inventory taken out

Why
Inventory is the hardest current asset to turn into cash.
Also called
The acid test ratio.

4 features to find

Find the four figures to pick, or leave out, for the liquidity ratios.

  1. Net receivables
  2. Total current assets
  3. Total current liabilities
  4. Loan left out

Net receivables

Why
Use receivables after the provision.

Total current assets

Work out
9 600 + 7 200 + 3 000 = 19 800.
Current ratio
19 800 / 6 000 = 3.30 : 1.

Total current liabilities

Work out
5 400 + 600 = 6 000.
Liquid ratio
(19 800 - 9 600) / 6 000 = 10 200 / 6 000 = 1.70 : 1.

Loan left out

Why
It is repayable after more than a year, so it is non-current.
Scores
Adding it gives the wrong ratio.

Find the three figures that each give a profitability ratio.

  1. Gross profit %
  2. Profit % of revenue
  3. Return on capital

Gross profit %

Work out
48 000 / 120 000 x 100 = 40%.

Profit % of revenue

Work out
18 000 / 120 000 x 100 = 15%.

Return on capital

Work out
18 000 / 150 000 x 100 = 12%.
Note
ROCE uses profit for the year, not gross profit.

2

The calculation

Liquid ratio = (Current assets − Inventory) ÷ CL

Current ratio = CA ÷ CL. Gross profit % = Gross profit ÷ Revenue × 100.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find CA:CA = liquid ratio × CL + I

To find CL:CL = (CA − I) ÷ liquid ratio

  • Liquidity: x : 1, not %.
  • Profitability ratios: x 100.
  • Use trade receivables after the provision.
  • Leave out long-term loans.

Try itCurrent assets $19 800 (inventory $9 600). Current liabilities $6 000. Give both to 2 d.p.

  1. Current ratio: 19 800 ÷ 6 000 = 3.30 : 1.
  2. Liquid assets: 19 800 − 9 600 = 10 200.
  3. Liquid ratio: 10 200 ÷ 6 000 = 1.70 : 1.

5

The method

  1. Write the formula

    Show the top and bottom, with x 100 for a %.

  2. Pick the figures

    Total current assets and current liabilities, using receivables after provision.

  3. Liquid ratio

    Take inventory away from current assets before dividing.

  4. Divide, round and form

    Round to the decimal places asked; use % or : 1 as the question needs.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong form

    No x 100 on a percentage, or a liquidity ratio written as a %.

  • ✗
    Gross receivables

    Use trade receivables after the provision.

  • ✗
    Loan in current liabilities

    A long-term loan is non-current. Leave it out.

  • ✗
    Inventory left in

    The liquid ratio always takes inventory out.

What a complete response includes

Each formula earns one mark. When a calculation is asked, the formula and the answer earn a mark each, and the answer can be credited as an own figure if the current assets or liabilities were slightly wrong.

(b) (i) Calculate Bob’s current (working capital) ratio and the liquid (acid test) ratio, stating the formula used. Answers should be given to two decimal places. (4)

View full original question and diagrams
Hint for part (b)(i)

Write the formula in words first, with the top and bottom clearly shown, then substitute the figures. Use trade receivables after the provision, leave long-term loans out of current liabilities, take inventory out for the liquid ratio, and round as the question asks.

Worked explanation

  1. Current assets: inventory 8 531; trade receivables 8 490 - 346 allowance = 8 144; other receivables 600; cash at bank 2 198; cash in hand 289. Total 19 762.
  2. Current liabilities: trade payables 4 320 + other payables 211 = 4 531. The bank loan (2028) is non-current, so it is left out.
  3. Current ratio: 19 762 / 4 531 = 4.3615..., so 4.36 : 1.
  4. Liquid assets: 19 762 - 8 531 = 11 231.
  5. Liquid ratio: 11 231 / 4 531 = 2.4787..., so 2.48 : 1.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Current (working capital) ratio Formula: current assets / current liabilities 19 762 / 4 531 = 4.36 : 1 Liquid (acid test) ratio Formula: (current assets - inventory) / current liabilities 11 231 / 4 531 = 2.48 : 1

How the marks are earned

One mark for each formula and one own-figure mark for each answer written to two decimal places as x : 1.

Calculation check

  • 19762 - 8531 = 11231
