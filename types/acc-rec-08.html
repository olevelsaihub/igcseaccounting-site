Why the petty cash in the box differs from the book

Written

The petty cash book balance should equal the cash counted in the box. If they differ, think of three kinds of cause: a recording error, loss or theft, or timing.

Book balance

Working
$150 - $112 = $38.
Meaning
This is how much cash should be in the box.

Cash actually there

Meaning
Only $26 is in the box.
Next step
Look for why the two figures do not agree.

The shortage

Working
$38 - $26 = $12.
Question to ask
Was $12 paid out and not recorded, or did it go missing?

Check the vouchers

Clue
A voucher may be missing or entered at the wrong amount.
Write it as
A payment was made but not entered in the book.

4 clues to find

Each cause is a different kind. Which kind is each one?

  1. Missing voucher
  2. Wrong amount
  3. Loss or theft
  4. Timing

Missing voucher

Why
Money left the box but nothing went in the book.
Write it as
A petty cash voucher has been lost, so a payment was not recorded.

Wrong amount

Why
$17 - $5 = $12 more was paid than the book shows.
Write it as
A payment was entered in the book at the wrong amount.

Loss or theft

Why
Physical cash can go missing.
Write it as
Cash has been stolen from the petty cash box.

Timing

Why
The cash moved before the paperwork.
Write it as
Cash was paid out but the voucher has not yet been entered.

Exam tipTwo answers about "an error in the book" count as one cause. Pick different kinds.

Does it score?

Exam question

State two reasons why the petty cash counted may be less than the petty cash book balance. (2 marks)

Decide first: would this answer earn the mark? Then tap it to check.

Tap an answer to see if it earns the mark.

4

The method

  1. Recording error

    Think of a voucher entered at the wrong amount or not entered at all.

  2. Loss or theft

    Think of cash that has been lost or stolen from the box.

  3. Timing differences

    Think of cash moving before or after the paperwork.

  4. One cause per sentence

    Write each cause as a separate, clear sentence.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Same cause twice

    "Error in the book" and "wrong entry" are one cause.

  • ✗
    Benefits instead of causes

    Answer the question asked: why the figures differ.

  • ✗
    Too vague

    Say what went wrong and where, not just "a mistake".

Worked explanation

  1. Cause 1 (recording): a payment slip was lost, so money left the box but nothing was entered in the book.
  2. Cause 2 (loss): some notes or coins were taken from the box.
  3. Check the two causes are different kinds.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

1 A petty cash voucher has been lost, so a payment was never entered in the book. 2 Cash has been stolen from the petty cash box.

How the marks are earned

One mark each for two different plausible causes. Other accepted causes include an error in the book and the top-up from the main cashier not yet received.
