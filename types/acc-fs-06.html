Where does this item go in the financial statements?

Written

Ask two questions. Is it income or an expense for the year (income statement), or owned or owed at year end (statement of financial position)? If owned or owed, is it current or non-current?

4

The method

  1. Year or year end?

    Year's income or expense: income statement. Owned or owed at year end: statement of financial position.

  2. Owned: how long?

    Used for more than a year is non-current; turned into cash soon is current.

  3. Owed: when due?

    Due within 12 months is current; due later is non-current.

  4. Deductions and one answer

    Put depreciation and provisions with the asset they reduce. Tick one column.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Deductions as liabilities

    Depreciation and provisions are not liabilities.

  • ✗
    Every loan as current

    Check the repayment date before you decide.

  • ✗
    Overdraft as an asset

    An overdraft is money owed to the bank: a current liability.

  • ✗
    Forgetting work in progress

    It is inventory, so it is a current asset.

Worked explanation

  1. Accumulated depreciation reduces machinery and other non-current assets, so it sits in the non-current assets section.
  2. The bank loan is repayable in 2026, more than a year after the 2019 year end, so it is a non-current liability.
  3. Work in progress is partly made inventory, so it is a current asset.
  4. A bank overdraft is repayable on demand, so it is a current liability.
  5. Other receivables (for example prepaid expenses) are short-term amounts due to the business: current assets.
  6. The provision for irrecoverable debts is deducted from trade receivables, so it appears under current assets.
