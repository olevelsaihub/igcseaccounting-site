Knowledge card

Public sector, private sector and sole traders

Written

Who owns an organisation decides how it is funded and what it is trying to achieve: the government runs the public sector to give a service, private owners run businesses to make a profit.

1

Who owns it, and why does it exist?

3 features to find

Find the three features that put this hospital in the public sector.

  1. Government ownership
  2. Funded from taxes
  3. Aim: a service

Government ownership

Why it matters
Ownership is what makes an organisation public or private.
Write it as
It is owned and run by the government.

Funded from taxes

Why it matters
Public sector bodies are paid for by the government, mainly from taxes.
Write it as
It is financed by the government from taxation.

Aim: a service

Why it matters
The aim is to serve the community, not to earn a profit.
Write it as
Its main aim is to provide a service to the community.

Exam tipThe number of staff is not a feature of the sector. Stick to ownership, funding and aim.

3 features to find

Find the three features that make Amira a sole trader.

  1. One owner
  2. Full control
  3. Keeps all profit

One owner

Why it matters
A sole trader is one person who owns the business.
Write it as
The business is owned by one person.

Full control

Advantage
Decisions can be made quickly, with no one to consult.
Write it as
The owner has full control and can make decisions quickly.

Keeps all profit

Advantage
The profit does not have to be shared.
Write it as
The owner keeps all of the profit.

Exam tip"Keeps all the profit" and "does not share profit" are the same point. Give a different advantage for the next mark.

4

The method

  1. Find the owner

    Ask who owns it: the government or private individuals.

  2. Link owner to aim

    Government means a service; private owners mean profit.

  3. Sole trader features

    Think of the single owner: full control, quick decisions, all profit kept, easy to set up.

  4. Write both sides

    For a difference, describe both organisations in one sentence joined by whereas.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Only one side

    A difference needs both organisations, or half the marks go.

  • ✗
    "Owned by the public"

    The public sector is owned by the government.

  • ✗
    Public limited company

    A plc is in the private sector. Do not mix it up with the public sector.

  • ✗
    Same point twice

    Each mark needs a different feature, not the same one reworded.

What a complete response includes

Each valid feature or advantage earns one mark. For a two-mark difference, one mark is for the point about the first organisation and one for the matching point about the second. Reasonable alternative points are accepted.

Worked explanation

  1. Read the question: it asks for one difference between the objectives (aims) of private sector and public sector organisations, for 2 marks.
  2. Private sector organisations are owned by private individuals, so their main aim is to make a profit.
  3. Public sector organisations are owned and funded by the government, so their main aim is to provide a service for the community.
  4. Join the two points in one sentence using 'whereas' so the examiner can see both sides.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Private sector organisations aim mainly to make a profit for their owners, whereas public sector organisations aim to provide a service to the community rather than to make a profit.

How the marks are earned

One mark for the private sector aim (making a profit) and one mark for the public sector aim (serving the community). Other sensible contrasts about objectives are also accepted, but each side must be stated.
