Preparing a statement of financial position after adjustments

Layout

The statement of financial position is a one-date snapshot: total assets equal total equity and liabilities. Show non-current assets at carrying amount and receivables after the provision.

2

The calculation

Total assets = Equity + Liabilities

Closing equity = Opening equity + Profit for the year − Drawings

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find A:A = E + L

To find E:E = A − L

To find L:L = A − E

Try itOpening equity is $150 000, profit for the year is $34 600 and drawings are $18 000. Liabilities total $22 650. What are total assets?

  1. Closing equity: 150 000 + 34 600 − 18 000 = 166 600.
  2. Total assets = equity + liabilities: 166 600 + 22 650.
  3. Total assets = $189 250.

5

The method

  1. Heading and non-current assets

    Name and date. Show cost, accumulated depreciation (old + this year), carrying amount.

  2. Current assets

    List inventory, receivables less the provision, other receivables, bank; total.

  3. Equity and liabilities

    Closing equity: opening + profit − drawings. Then liabilities, including accruals.

  4. Check it balances

    Total equity and liabilities must equal total assets.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Last year's depreciation

    Add this year's charge to the old accumulated depreciation.

  • ✗
    Provision left out

    Deduct the provision from trade receivables.

  • ✗
    Missing accruals and prepayments

    Show them as other payables and other receivables.

  • ✗
    Overdraft as an asset

    A credit bank balance is a current liability.

What a complete response includes

Marks go to each correctly valued line (the carrying amounts, net trade receivables, the accrual, the overdraft) and to the profit figure carried in from the income statement, which can be own figure. Section totals, closing equity and both grand totals are own-figure marks, so the layout and the totals matter as much as the single figures.

Worked explanation

  1. Profit for the year (from part (a)): income 419 250 + 800 gain on disposal + 150 fall in provision = 420 200; expenses 10 800 + 250 + 39 630 + 252 000 + 5 000 + 2 560 = 310 240; profit 109 960.
  2. Wages: 231 000 covers 11 months, so one month is 21 000; the year is 252 000 and March's 21 000 is owing (other payables).
  3. Fixtures and fittings: depreciation 10% x 50 000 = 5 000; accumulated 10 000 + 5 000 = 15 000; carrying amount 35 000.
  4. Motor vehicles: depreciation (20 000 - 7 200) x 20% = 2 560; accumulated 7 200 + 2 560 = 9 760; carrying amount 10 240.
  5. Land and buildings are not depreciated: 250 000. Non-current assets total 250 000 + 35 000 + 10 240 = 295 240.
  6. Trade receivables: provision 5% x 12 000 = 600, so 12 000 - 600 = 11 400.
  7. Current assets: 35 200 + 11 400 + 1 120 = 47 720. Total assets: 295 240 + 47 720 = 342 960.
  8. Closing equity: 250 000 + 109 960 - 50 000 = 309 960.
  9. Current liabilities: trade payables 9 800 + wages owing 21 000 + bank overdraft 2 200 (the credit bank balance) = 33 000.
  10. Total equity and liabilities: 309 960 + 33 000 = 342 960, which equals total assets.

How the marks are earned

The carrying amounts of fixtures and fittings and motor vehicles earn a mark each, and the non-current assets total another. Net trade receivables earn a mark, the current assets total and total assets are own-figure marks. The profit for the year carries two marks (own figure from part (a)), closing equity is an own figure, and the wages accrual and the bank overdraft earn a mark each, with the final total as an own figure.
