Knowledge card

Finding equity and profit when records are incomplete

Calculation

When full records are not kept, equity = total assets − total liabilities, and the profit is the increase in equity, after adding back drawings and taking off any capital introduced.

  • Equity (capital) = total assets - total liabilities. This is the accounting equation rearranged.
  • A statement of affairs is a list of assets and liabilities at a date, used to find equity when full records are not kept.
  • Non-current assets are included at carrying amount (cost less accumulated depreciation).
  • A bank overdraft is a liability, not an asset.
  • Profit = closing equity - opening equity + drawings - extra capital introduced.
  • If closing equity is lower than expected after these adjustments, the result is a loss.

Working

Why it matters
This is not yet the profit.

Add back drawings

Why it matters
Drawings reduced equity, but they are not a loss. Adding them back shows what was earned.

Take off new capital

Why it matters
New capital raised equity, but it was not earned by trading.

Check

The calculation

Profit = Closing equity − Opening equity + Drawings − Capital introduced

Find each equity first: Equity = Total assets − Total liabilities.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find P:P = CE − OE + D − CI

To find CE:CE = OE + P + CI − D

To find OE:OE = CE − P + D − CI

  • Use carrying amounts, not cost, for non-current assets.
  • A bank overdraft is a liability, so it reduces equity.
  • Drawings are added back; new capital is taken off.

Try itDina's assets at 1 January 2025 total $39 000 and liabilities total $7 200. Equity at 31 December 2025 is $42 600. Drawings were $14 000 and she introduced $5 000. Find the profit.

  1. Opening equity: 39 000 - 7 200 = 31 800.
  2. Increase in equity: 42 600 - 31 800 = 10 800.
  3. Add back drawings: 10 800 + 14 000 = 24 800.
  4. Take off capital introduced: 24 800 - 5 000 = $19 800 profit.

5

The method

  1. Sort the balances

    Put each balance under assets or liabilities.

  2. Carrying amounts

    Take accumulated depreciation off the cost of each non-current asset.

  3. Two totals

    Add up total assets and total liabilities separately, and show both.

  4. Equity

    Equity = total assets - total liabilities.

  5. Profit

    Closing equity - opening equity, then add drawings and take off capital introduced.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Assets at cost

    Use cost less accumulated depreciation.

  • ✗
    Overdraft as an asset

    A bank overdraft is a liability.

  • ✗
    Forgetting drawings

    Add drawings back when finding profit.

  • ✗
    New capital as profit

    Deduct capital introduced; it was not earned.

  • ✗
    No workings

    Show the totals so marks can be given for each step.
