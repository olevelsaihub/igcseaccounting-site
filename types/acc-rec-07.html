Knowledge card

Why keep a petty cash book

Written

A petty cash book keeps many small cash payments out of the main cash book. This saves the chief cashier's time, lets a junior member of staff do the work, and helps the owner control small spending.

1

Why bother with a petty cash book?

4 reasons to find

Find the benefits of keeping a petty cash book in this office.

  1. Fewer main entries
  2. Delegation and training
  3. Control of spending
  4. Spending by type

Fewer main entries

Benefit
46 small payments stay out of the main cash book.
Write it as
The main cash book has fewer entries, so it is easier to use and check.

Delegation and training

Benefit
The chief cashier hands small payments to a junior.
Write it as
This saves the chief cashier's time and trains the junior clerk.

Control of spending

Benefit
The fixed float limits how much can be spent.
Write it as
The imprest system helps the owner check and control small spending.

Spending by type

Benefit
Spending is grouped by type.
Write it as
Column totals can be posted to the ledger in one go.

Exam tipEach mark needs a different benefit. Do not give the same one twice in new words.

3 reasons to find

Does Ben need a petty cash book? Find the points against it.

  1. No one to delegate to
  2. Few small payments
  3. Risk of loss

No one to delegate to

Point
With no staff, there is no junior to hand the work to.
Effect
One of the main benefits is lost.

Few small payments

Drawback
Keeping a second book is extra work for only 3 payments.
Write it as
The extra work may cost more time than it saves.

Risk of loss

Drawback
A separate float is more cash that could be lost or stolen.
Judgement
Ben probably does not need a petty cash book.

Exam tipA discuss answer needs both sides and a conclusion about this business.

4

The method

  1. Pick a benefit

    Fewer main cash book entries, delegation, training, control of spending, or analysis.

  2. Develop it

    For explain, add the effect with so or which means.

  3. Give a drawback

    For discuss, also give a drawback such as extra work or the risk of theft.

  4. Conclude with a judgement

    End a discuss answer with a clear judgement linked to this business.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Same benefit twice

    Reworded points earn one mark only. Give a different benefit.

  • ✗
    Describing the layout

    Say why the book is kept, not what its columns are.

  • ✗
    No drawback or conclusion

    A discuss answer needs both sides and a judgement.

  • ✗
    Wrong book

    Do not confuse the petty cash book with the main cash book.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Think about who benefits: the main cashier, junior staff and the owner.
  2. Purpose 1: it keeps lots of tiny payments out of the main cash book.
  3. Purpose 2: it lets the owner keep track of, and limit, small office spending.
  4. Check the two points are different.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

1 To keep many small payments out of the main cash book, so that book has fewer entries. 2 To keep a check on small office expenses, so spending on items like postage and cleaning is controlled.

How the marks are earned

One mark per different purpose, up to two. Other accepted purposes include easing the main cashier's workload, recording small cash spending, and giving a junior member of staff training.
