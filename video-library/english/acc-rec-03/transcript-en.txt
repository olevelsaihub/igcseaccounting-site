Idea. A café buys a hundred cartons of milk every week. Its supplier gives it a lower price than a shopper buying one carton, and a little extra off if it pays quickly. The first is trade discount: money off the list price for trade and bulk buyers. Twenty per cent off one thousand dollars means the customer owes eight hundred. Only that net amount goes in the books. The second is cash discount: money off for paying within an agreed time, such as five per cent within thirty days. It has nothing to do with paying in notes and coins.

Method. Step one: check which discount the question names. Trade discount is about the size and loyalty of orders. Cash discount is about the timing of payment. Trade discount brings bigger orders, loyal customers, and higher sales. Cash discount brings cash in sooner, and less risk that a debt is never paid. Write each benefit as a separate short point.

Worked example. Here is a real question from June 2025. State two benefits to a business of offering a trade discount. Two marks. Trade discount is for trade and bulk buyers. Benefit one: a lower price on big orders persuades customers to buy in larger quantities. Benefit two: a good price keeps customers loyal, so they come back with repeat orders. These are two different ideas, so two marks. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is giving a cash discount reason in a trade discount question. Paying sooner is about cash discount. Another is thinking cash discount means paying in notes and coins. It means paying within the time limit.

Your turn. Now you try. Bright Paints sells to builders on credit, and offers three per cent cash discount for payment within fourteen days. State two benefits of this to Bright Paints. Pause here and work it out.

Answer. This is cash discount, so think about timing. Benefit one: customers pay sooner, so Bright Paints has more cash to run the business. Benefit two: debts are cleared quickly, so there is less risk that a customer never pays. That means fewer irrecoverable debts. Remember: check which discount is named, think about bulk or timing, and give one different benefit per mark. Now try the practice questions.
