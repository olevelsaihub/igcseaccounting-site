Why non-current assets lose value (causes of depreciation)

Read the narration

Idea. Think of a new phone. Two years later, the battery is weaker and the screen is scratched. Newer models have also come out, so it looks old-fashioned. It is worth much less than you paid. Business assets are the same. Depreciation is the fall in value of a non-current asset, such as machinery, motor vehicles or equipment, over its useful life. There are four causes. Wear and tear, from using the asset. Passage of time, as when a lease runs out. Obsolescence, when it goes out of date. And depletion, when a natural resource is used up.

Method. Count how many causes the question asks for. Pick that many from the four, and write each on its own numbered line, in a few words. Link each cause to an example. A van's tyres wear out: wear and tear. A ten-year lease runs out: passage of time. An old computer: obsolescence. A quarry being emptied: depletion.

Worked example. Here is a real question from June 2025. State two reasons why non-current assets depreciate. Two marks, so we need two different causes. Think of a machine in a factory. The more it is used, the more it wears out. That is cause one: wear and tear. Now think of a computer. Newer models make it out of date, even if it still works. That is cause two: obsolescence. Write each one on its own line. Passage of time or depletion would also be correct. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is giving the purpose of depreciation, spreading the cost, instead of a cause. The question asks why the value falls. Another is writing the same cause twice in different words. Usage and wear and tear are one idea, so they earn only one mark.

Your turn. Now you try. Read about the taxi company, and give three different causes of depreciation, each with an example from the business. Pause here and work it out.

Answer. Wear and tear: the cars wear out because they are driven every day. Obsolescence: the booking system goes out of date when newer software appears. Passage of time: the five-year lease runs out. Remember: four causes, wear and tear, passage of time, obsolescence and depletion. Give different ideas, one per line. Now try the practice questions.
