Idea. When you tell a friend a secret, you trust them to keep it, to be honest with you, and to be fair. Businesses trust their accountants in the same way. These rules are called the principles of professional ethics. There are five: integrity, objectivity, professional competence, confidentiality, and professional behaviour.

Method. Learn one key word for each. Integrity means being honest and straightforward. Objectivity means no bias: you make your own fair judgement, without pressure from others. Professional competence means keeping your knowledge and skills up to date, and working carefully. Confidentiality means not sharing information without permission, unless the law requires it. Professional behaviour means obeying the law. In a question, find the key word in the description, such as honest, bias, knowledge, or private information. Match it to a principle, and write its full name. If you are asked which is not a principle, first cross out accounting concepts, such as prudence, and made-up words, such as duality.

Worked example. Here is a real question from June 2025. It gives two descriptions, and we must name the principle of professional ethics for each. Two marks. Row one says accountants must be free from bias or conflict of interest. The key word is bias. So the principle is objectivity. Row two says accountants must act in a straightforward and honest way. The key word is honest. So the principle is integrity. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. The most common mistake is swapping integrity and objectivity. Integrity is about honesty. Objectivity is about bias. Another is giving an accounting concept, such as prudence, as an ethics principle. That scores nothing.

Your turn. Now you try. Read the two cases, and name the principle of professional ethics in each. Pause here and work it out.

Answer. Case one: Lina keeps a client's private information to herself. That is confidentiality. Case two: Omar keeps his knowledge and skills up to date. That is professional competence. Remember: find the key word, match it to one of the five principles, and write its full name. Now try the practice questions.
