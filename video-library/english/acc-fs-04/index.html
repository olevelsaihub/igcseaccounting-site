Calculating a revised profit

Idea. You think you made fifty dollars washing cars this weekend. Then you remember you still owe five dollars for soap, and ten dollars was paid for next week's wash. Your real earnings are thirty-five dollars. A business does the same. Its first profit figure is the draft profit. After year-end adjustments or corrections, it becomes the revised profit.

Method. Write the draft profit at the top. For each item, ask: does it add an expense, or take one away? Does it add income, or take it away? That gives you the sign. Only adjust the part that belongs to this year. If a three-month bill of nine hundred dollars has two months in this year, only six hundred counts. Put each item on its own line, with brackets for a deduction. Then add up, and label the final figure revised profit for the year.

Worked example. Here is a real question from June 2025. Calculate Ruhi's revised profit for the year ended thirty-first March 2025. Five marks. The draft profit is thirty-seven thousand eight hundred. Four items were left out. Note the trade receivables of twelve thousand three hundred. We need them for the provision. Rental income received in advance belongs to next year. It is not this year's income, so take it off: minus six hundred and fifty. Goods taken for own use come out of purchases. Cost of sales falls, so profit rises: plus two hundred and eighty. Depreciation was understated by four hundred. That is an extra expense, so minus four hundred. The new provision is two per cent of twelve thousand three hundred: two hundred and forty-six. Another expense, so minus. Thirty-seven thousand eight hundred, minus six hundred and fifty, plus two hundred and eighty, minus four hundred, minus two hundred and forty-six. The revised profit is thirty-six thousand, seven hundred and eighty-four dollars. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is taking goods for own use off profit. Recording them lowers cost of sales, so profit goes up. Another is using the whole receivables balance as the provision. Use the percentage only.
