Idea. Think about a local café. The owner, the staff, the bank that lent it money, and the supplier who delivers the milk all care how the café is doing. Anyone with an interest in how a business is doing is a stakeholder. Internal stakeholders are inside it: the owner, managers and employees. External stakeholders are outside it, such as customers, suppliers and banks.

Method. Each stakeholder reads the accounts to make a decision. So ask: what does this group need to know? The owner: is it making a profit? A supplier: will we be paid? A bank: can the loan be repaid? An employee asks: is my job safe? A customer asks: will supplies continue? The government asks: how much tax is due on the profit? Be specific. To see how the business is doing is too vague to score. And use a different stakeholder for each point.

Worked example. Here is a real question from June 2019. It asks for two stakeholders and their interests. Employees are already given as the example. Four marks. We must not repeat employees. Choose a supplier. It sells goods to the business on credit, so it wants to know whether the business can pay what it owes. Next, a bank that has lent money. It wants to know whether the business can repay the loan and the interest on time. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is repeating the example already in the question. Employees were given, so they earn nothing. Another is a vague interest, such as to see how the business is doing. Say exactly what they want to know.

Your turn. Now you try. Zara owns a gym. State two external stakeholders and the interest of each in the gym's financial statements. Pause here and work it out.

Answer. External means outside the business. The bank that lent Zara money wants to know if the loan and interest can be repaid. The supplier of the equipment wants to know if it will be paid. Customers, asking whether the gym will stay open, would also be a good answer. Remember: name a stakeholder, think about the decision it makes, and give a specific interest.
