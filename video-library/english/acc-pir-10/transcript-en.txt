Idea. Your savings jar has fifty dollars at the start of the year. You add twenty of birthday money, earn thirty from chores, and spend fifteen on yourself. At the end you have eighty-five dollars. A trader's equity changes the same way. Closing equity is opening equity, plus additional capital, plus profit, minus drawings. This is the equity section of the statement of financial position.

Method. First, find opening equity: opening assets minus opening liabilities. Then show non-current assets at carrying amount, after this year's depreciation, including any asset added this year. Classify each loan by its repayment date. Due within twelve months: a current liability. Due later: non-current. An overdraft is always current. If profit is not given, it is the figure that makes the statement balance. Total equity is total assets minus total liabilities. Then work back to profit.

Worked example. Here is a real question from June 2024. Prepare Thelma's statement of financial position at thirty-first December 2023. Fifteen marks. Here are Thelma's balances at the end of the year: the bank overdraft, cash in hand, inventory, receivables and payables. Read what happened during the year. Thelma paid for a second vehicle with her own money. Every vehicle is depreciated at twenty per cent, straight line. A new loan is due in June 2024. And she took out cash and goods. Opening equity comes from part (a)(i): twelve thousand dollars. Vehicles now cost twelve thousand plus ten thousand: twenty-two thousand. Straight-line depreciation at twenty per cent is four thousand four hundred, leaving seventeen thousand six hundred. Current assets are seventeen thousand eight hundred and twenty-five. Total assets are thirty-five thousand four hundred and twenty-five. The loan repayable in 2028 is non-current: five thousand. The loan due in June 2024 is current, with the overdraft and payables: eight thousand and ninety. Total liabilities are thirteen thousand and ninety. Total equity must be thirty-five thousand four hundred and twenty-five minus thirteen thousand and ninety: twenty-two thousand three hundred and thirty-five. Drawings are four thousand cash plus eight hundred of goods. Working back, profit is five thousand one hundred and thirty-five. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is forgetting goods taken for own use. They are drawings too. Another is putting a loan due within a year under non-current liabilities. Check the date.
