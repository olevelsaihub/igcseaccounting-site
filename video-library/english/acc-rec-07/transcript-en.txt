Idea. Think of a busy office. Someone buys stamps, pays a taxi fare, or buys cleaning materials. These are tiny cash payments, and there are lots of them. Writing every one in the main cash book would fill it with clutter. So businesses keep a petty cash book. Petty means small. It records small cash payments made from a float kept in the office, separate from the main cash book. Why keep one? It keeps the main cash book free of small entries. It saves the chief cashier's time and trains a junior. It controls small spending, and its analysis columns group the spending by type.

Method. First, choose a purpose from the list. Each mark needs a different idea, so never say the same thing twice in other words. If the question says explain, add the effect on the business. For example: a junior handles small payments, so the chief cashier has more time for larger items. If the question says discuss, give a drawback too, such as extra work or the risk of cash being lost or stolen. Then make a judgement for that business. A very small business may not need one.

Worked example. Here is a real question from November 2024. State two purposes of maintaining a petty cash book. Two marks, so we need two different ideas. Purpose one: to keep many small payments out of the main cash book, so that book has fewer entries. Purpose two: to keep a check on small office expenses, so spending on things like postage and cleaning is controlled. Check that the two points are different. One is about the main cash book, the other is about control. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is giving the same purpose twice in different words. Saving the cashier's time and making the cashier less busy are one idea. Another is describing what the book looks like, instead of why it is kept.

Answer. One answer: the receptionist can make the small payments from petty cash, so Omar's chief cashier has more time for the larger payments. Another good answer is fewer entries in the main cash book, so it is easier to check. Remember: choose different purposes, develop each with so, and for discuss give a drawback and a judgement.
