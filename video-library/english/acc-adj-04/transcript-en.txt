Idea. A van loses value every year it is used. Accountants measure that fall with a yearly depreciation charge. There are two methods: straight line and reducing balance. Straight line gives the same charge every year. Take the cost, minus the residual value, the amount it is expected to sell for at the end, and divide by its useful life. A twenty thousand dollar van, worth four thousand at the end of four years, loses four thousand a year. Reducing balance takes a percentage of the carrying value at the start of each year. At twenty per cent, year one is four thousand, year two is three thousand two hundred, and the charge keeps falling.

Method. First, write down the facts for each asset: cost, residual value, rate or life, and the method. Then read the time policy, for example a full year in the year of purchase. Next, list the financial years from purchase to the date asked. If the year ends on 30 April, a machine bought on 1 May 2020 is charged for the years ending April 2021, then April 2022. Last, add the charges to get accumulated depreciation, and take that from cost to get the carrying value. For the van on reducing balance, after two years: twenty thousand minus seven thousand two hundred, twelve thousand eight hundred.

Worked example. Here is a real question from June 2023. Calculate the depreciation charge for each machine for each year. Four marks. Xavier bought two machines on 1 May 2020. Machine A cost twenty-eight thousand, and machine B thirty-six thousand. He uses reducing balance at thirty per cent, with a full year's charge in the year of purchase. Machine A. Thirty per cent of twenty-eight thousand is eight thousand four hundred. That leaves nineteen thousand six hundred. Thirty per cent of that is five thousand eight hundred and eighty. Machine B. Thirty per cent of thirty-six thousand is ten thousand eight hundred. That leaves twenty-five thousand two hundred. Thirty per cent of that is seven thousand five hundred and sixty. So the four charges are eight thousand four hundred and five thousand eight hundred and eighty for A, and ten thousand eight hundred and seven thousand five hundred and sixty for B. Common mistake. A common mistake is taking the reducing balance rate of the cost every year. That is straight line. Year two must use the reduced value. Another is forgetting to take off the residual value under straight line.

Your turn. Now you try. Mina bought equipment and uses reducing balance. Find the charge for 2025 and the carrying value at the end of 2025. Pause here and work it out.

Answer. In 2024, twenty per cent of fifteen thousand is three thousand, leaving twelve thousand. In 2025, twenty per cent of twelve thousand is two thousand four hundred. That answers part a. Accumulated depreciation is three thousand plus two thousand four hundred, five thousand four hundred. Fifteen thousand minus that gives a carrying value of nine thousand six hundred dollars. Remember: write the facts, count the years, charge each year with the right method, then take accumulated depreciation from cost. Now try the practice questions.
