Idea. A shop sells to hundreds of credit customers each month. Posting every invoice to the sales account would take ages. So the sales day book lists them, and only its total is posted. Sales, purchases and expenses are nominal accounts. Sales is income, so its totals go on the credit side. Purchases and expenses go on the debit side, including each petty cash analysis column total.

Method. Put the opening balance on its normal side. Work out any book totals, for example the total to date plus new entries. Post each total on the same side, with the date and the name of the book. Leave out returns. Then close the account. For a year-end transfer, write income statement on the other side, so both sides total the same. If the question says balance the account, use balance carried down and brought down instead.

Worked example. Here is a real question from June 2023, Paper 1. The sales account balance on the first of March was seventy-one thousand seven hundred and thirty dollars. Prepare the sales account, showing the transfer to the income statement. Five marks. In March, Mitch sold goods on credit to Jay and to Mateta, Jay returned goods, and there was a cash sale of fifty dollars. The sales day book total to date was three thousand one hundred and ninety-four. Add the new credit sales to the total to date: three thousand one hundred and ninety-four, plus five hundred and seventy, plus three hundred and sixteen, is four thousand and eighty. Sales is income, so the opening balance goes on the credit side. Post the day book total and the cash sale, also on the credit side. Jay's return of eighty-five dollars goes to the sales returns account, not here. The credit side totals seventy-five thousand eight hundred and sixty. Transfer it to the income statement on the debit side, so both sides agree. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is debiting the sales day book total. Sales is income, so it is a credit. Another is writing balance carried down when the question asks for the transfer. The label must be income statement.
