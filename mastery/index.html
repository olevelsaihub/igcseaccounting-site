Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Accounting · 4AC1

Block mastery checks

A short check after every block, with new questions on every attempt.

After every block, check that you have really mastered it. Each check takes two new questions from every type in the block (at least 10 questions), marks them instantly and explains every answer. Every retake gives you a new set. The questions are written by olevels.com for practice; they are not past-paper questions.

  1. Take the block check. If you score 90% or more, you are ready to move on.
  2. Below 90%? Your result lists each type as Secure or Revisit this type. Rework the ones to revisit.
  3. Retake the check until you reach at least 90%. Aim for 100%.

Scores are saved in this browser only.
