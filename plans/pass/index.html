The review covered 36 current component papers from 2019–2025.

Current-paper marks were assigned to each part’s sole primary type, and every selected type’s prerequisites were added.

Historical coverage check

18 of 18 current paper pairs cleared the target

The route covered at least 97/150 primary-owned marks on every complete 2019–2025 pair. Each pair stayed at least 10 marks above the higher of its own historical Grade 5 boundary and the matching June 2026 Grade 5 boundary.

This is 100% historical paper-pair coverage, not a 100% probability claim for a future student grade. Accuracy, timing, independent recall and unseen wording still matter.

Completion time

8 learning days plus 6 paper sessions

Study 7 types per day, then sit three complete Paper 1 + Paper 2 mock pairs as six separate paper sessions. That is 14 active days.

This keeps the olevels.com Pass Plan pace of about 6.6 types per learning day.

Readiness gate

Completion is practice. Fresh papers confirm readiness.

After completing the plan, attempt three fresh, unseen 150-mark Paper 1 + Paper 2 paper pairs under timed conditions. Have each pair independently marked with its official scheme. Grade 5 readiness is supported only when every score is at least 10 marks above that exact pair’s published Grade 5 boundary.

Supporting skills for Accounting

Use these short bridges when a starting skill needs strengthening.

Reading an exam question and its data

Identify the business, date range and task; pick out the given figures and adjustments; and match the answer form (list, account, statement, calculation, written explanation) to the command word.

Arithmetic for accounting

Add, subtract, multiply and divide money amounts accurately; work out percentages and percentage reductions (e.g. trade discount, depreciation rates); use fractions and ratios to share amounts; round sensibly and check totals.

Accounting layouts

Set out headings and dates; use debit/credit (T and running-balance) account formats with balance c/d and b/d; rule off and total columns; and lay out multi-column books and statements with sub-totals.
