Ratio analysis: Ratio formulas and how to calculate them · Evaluating profitability using ratios · Liquidity and working capital

Partnerships: Should a trader form a partnership (or an LLP)? · Why partners get interest, salaries and loan interest · Partnership appropriation account · Partners' current and capital accounts

Manufacturing accounts: The three inventories of a manufacturer · Preparing and correcting a manufacturing account · Preparing a manufacturer's income statement
