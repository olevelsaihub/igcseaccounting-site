Before you start: supporting skills

These starting skills support many types. Check them first, and come back to them whenever a type feels hard.

  • Reading an exam question and its data: Identify the business, date range and task; pick out the given figures and adjustments; and match the answer form (list, account, statement, calculation, written explanation) to the command word.
  • Arithmetic for accounting: Add, subtract, multiply and divide money amounts accurately; work out percentages and percentage reductions (e.g. trade discount, depreciation rates); use fractions and ratios to share amounts; round sensibly and check totals.
  • Accounting layouts: Set out headings and dates; use debit/credit (T and running-balance) account formats with balance c/d and b/d; rule off and total columns; and lay out multi-column books and statements with sub-totals.
